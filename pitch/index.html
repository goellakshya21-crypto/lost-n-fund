CLAIM No. 02
The problem

Regulars don't leave loudly

A kirana's best customers drift to another shop, a quick-commerce app or cash. The owner notices weeks later, if ever. The payments app sees the drop in its data and does nothing with it.

An app-level churn model calls them safe: they still pay on the app, just not at this shop.

CLAIM No. 03
Why today's tools miss it

Three blind spots

Churn models watch the app

They flag people leaving the app. A regular can stop coming to a shop and keep paying on the app everywhere else.

Blanket offers pay the wrong people

A regular who is travelling, or on a normal gap for them, comes back anyway. An offer buys nothing there.

Shops can't see who left

Sharing a customer's name with the shop would break NPCI and DPDP rules, so the shop can't act alone.

CLAIM No. 04
The insight

Not every slipping regular needs an offer

Each one gets a diagnosed cause, and the cause decides who acts. Only the causes an offer can fix get one.

The rest get a payments fix, a wait, an alert to merchant ops, or nothing.

CLAIM No. 05
How it works

From a payment drop to a measured win-back

STEP 1

Score

Every customer-shop pair gets a calibrated chance of lapsing at that shop.

STEP 2

Diagnose

Moved shop, switched app, payments failing, travelling, or a normal gap?

STEP 3

Route

Pick the fix and who pays for it. The fix may be no offer at all.

STEP 4

Offer

Cashback sized to the customer's rhythm, sent only if it pays for whoever funds it.

STEP 5

Measure

Every run keeps a holdout, so the effect is measured against people who got nothing.

Uses consented adults only. Pharmacies are excluded. Data stays in India.

CLAIM No. 06
Offer design

Cashback, not a discount at the counter

Nothing changes at the counter

The customer pays the full bill and the Soundbox announces the full amount. No disputes over a short payment.

The shop never sees who

Its share comes off its settlement as one weekly line, so no single payment reveals who was picked.

Abuse is easy to stop

Paid after the payment clears: minimum bill, one qualifying payment a day, streak days, clawback on refunds.

Any funder can pay

The shop, Paytm, or both split one reward. A price cut at the counter can't do that.

CLAIM No. 08
The model

Probabilities you can price with

The offer economics multiply by this probability, so it has to be right. A model prices offers only after it passes these checks.

Built and tested on simulated data so far. Its real accuracy is measured on pilot data, and only then reported.

  • Out-of-time test: trained on past snapshots, scored on a later month it never saw
  • Beats a simple baseline: compared with a logistic regression on the same pairs
  • Calibrated: predicted and actual lapse rates checked decile by decile, cross-fitted so it isn't graded on its own homework
  • Promoted only if it isn't worse: otherwise the new model runs as a shadow
  • Watched every run: input drift and kill switches on opt-outs, complaints and budget burn

CLAIM No. 10
Measured against a holdout

Measured against people who got nothing

Every run holds back a random group of eligible customers. The win-back effect is the gap between the two groups, not a before-and-after story.

If a sample is too small to prove an effect, the report says so, and weekly runs are pooled until it can.
